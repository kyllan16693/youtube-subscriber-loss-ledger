History & privacy

Counts update when you visit or look up a channel. This device keeps up to 120 recent observations per channel, for 100 channels. Incognito lookups are not recorded.

Public YouTube requests omit account cookies. Saved channel names, counts, and observation times stay in your browser. Source links open when you click them.

Lifetime unsubscribes cannot be reconstructed from public subscriber totals. Published loss records retain their original dates and scope.
