# Privacy policy: YouTube Subscriber Loss Ledger

Effective date: October 24, 2026. Publisher: Kyllan Wunder.

## What the extension accesses

On YouTube channel pages, or when you use the popup or lookup page, the extension reads the channel URL and requests the public YouTube channel About page. It extracts the public channel ID, name, canonical URL, displayed subscriber count and its rounding information, and available public view/video counts and join-date text. The popup reads the active tab URL when opened. It does not read private YouTube Analytics, passwords, comments, or video viewing history, and does not request access to your Google account. Public metadata requests omit account cookies.

## What stays on your device

Recording is enabled by default. Chrome's local extension storage saves settings and public channel observations: channel ID, name, URL, displayed subscriber count, precision information, and observation time. It retains at most 100 channels and 120 recent observations per channel, with older records rolling off. It does not use Chrome Sync. Incognito lookups are not recorded. A small in-memory cache of public channel metadata expires after ten minutes and is bounded to 100 entries.

## Network requests and sharing

The extension sends channel-page requests to YouTube to provide lookups. YouTube receives ordinary request information, such as the requested URL and network connection information, under its own policies. The extension has no publisher-operated server, analytics, advertising, telemetry, paid data provider, or remote code. The publisher does not receive the locally saved observations and does not sell or share them. Public source reports are bundled as attributed historical summaries. Their websites are contacted only if you open a citation, using your normal browser.

## Your controls

Open Lookup & history to pause recording, forget a saved channel, or export local observations to a JSON file. Exported files are saved where you choose and are not automatically uploaded. Uninstalling the extension removes its Chrome local storage; files you export remain until you delete them. There is no scheduled background polling; observations update when you visit or look up a channel.

## Purpose and changes

Data is used only to identify the channel, display cited public loss reports, and maintain your local public-count history. The extension does not infer exact lifetime unsubscribes from public counts. Any material changes to these practices will be documented here with a new effective date.

For questions or reports, use https://github.com/kyllan16693/youtube-subscriber-loss-ledger/issues . GitHub is a separate service; do not include private browsing history or other sensitive information in a public issue.
